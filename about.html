My story

For six years, I worked as a Senior Software Developer at a leading IT company in Bangalore, building products and websites for corporate clients. I saw first-hand what quality web development looks like — clean code, proper SEO, real design thinking.

When I moved back home, I noticed something: most small businesses — restaurants, shops, startups, schools, clinics — either had no website at all, or were stuck with outdated, template-heavy sites that didn't actually bring in customers. Meanwhile, agencies were quoting far more than a small business could justify, and taking months to deliver.

So I started Budget Website Developers — bringing city-level technical skill directly to businesses, at prices that make sense, with the speed and personal attention a big agency can't offer. No account managers, no outsourced developers, no delays. You talk to me, and I build your site myself.

Where every site on this page actually gets built — no team, no outsourcing, just this desk.
