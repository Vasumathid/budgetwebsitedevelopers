My story

For six years, I worked as a Senior Software Developer at a leading IT company in Bangalore, building products and websites for corporate clients. I saw first-hand what quality web development looks like — clean code, proper SEO, real design thinking.

When I moved back to Vellore, I noticed something: most schools, clinics and local businesses across Tamil Nadu either had no website at all, or were stuck with outdated, template-heavy sites that didn't actually bring in customers. Meanwhile, agencies were quoting ₹40,000–₹80,000 and taking months to deliver.

So I started Budget Website Developers — bringing city-level technical skill directly to local businesses, at prices that make sense for Tamil Nadu, with the speed and personal attention a big agency can't offer. No account managers, no outsourced developers, no delays. You talk to me, and I build your site myself.
