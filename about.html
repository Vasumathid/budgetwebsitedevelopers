My story

For six years, I worked as a Senior Software Developer at Microchip Technology in Bangalore. Working in a professional software environment taught me something I still carry into every project: good technology should be reliable, purposeful and built properly — not simply made to look good.

After moving back to Tamil Nadu, I started noticing a different problem. Many good businesses had built something valuable offline, but their online presence did not reflect the quality of what they actually offered.

That gap is why I started Budget Website Developers — to make professional web development more accessible to businesses that don't need a huge agency, but still deserve serious work.

Where I work on client projects — directly, without unnecessary layers.

What working with me actually means

You don't have to explain your requirements to one person, wait for them to pass it to another person, and then wait again. You speak directly with me. I understand the requirement, build the website, show you the work, make the agreed changes and help with the handover.

I'm intentionally keeping the business founder-led. The goal isn't to become another agency with layers. The goal is to understand each project properly and deliver work I can stand behind.

I bring the same approach from software development into websites: understand the problem, learn what is needed, take responsibility for the work and finish it properly.

🎯 Mission

Help good businesses present themselves professionally online without making web development unnecessarily complicated or expensive.

🔭 Vision

The principles behind my work

Honesty over hype

No inflated numbers or unnecessary urgency. I believe the information you receive should be clear enough to make your own decision.

Craft over templates

Your website should reflect your business, customers and goals rather than feel like a generic online template.

Access over layers

You communicate directly with me, so requirements and feedback do not have to pass through unnecessary layers.

Fair pricing, local reality

The aim is professional work at a sensible price, without adding costs that do not improve the website.

Solve the real problem, not just the brief

A good website should solve the business problem behind the request — not just complete a checklist of pages and features.
